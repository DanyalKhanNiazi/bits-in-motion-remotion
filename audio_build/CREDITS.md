**Pixabay Content License:**
- Licence summary: https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/.
- Binding terms: https://pixabay.com/service/terms/.
- What it allows: commercial use, and modifying or adapting, without attribution.
- What it forbids: distributing the music as a standalone file.
- Content ID: none of the four Pixabay tracks (Style 6 re-uses the Style 3 track) showed a "Content ID Registered" label when checked. Keep the download page, the date and the licence summary as proof of permitted use.
- Possible exposure, Style 1: the uploader also links to AudioJungle and Envato portfolios. Enrolment of this recording elsewhere could neither be confirmed nor ruled out (medium risk, unverified).

**Kevin MacLeod, "Stay the Course" (Style 4): the credit line.**

incompetech's FAQ (https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/faq.html) gives this template, to be placed in the video description:

> "Stay the Course" Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com)
> Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0
> https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

- **Changes.** CC BY 4.0 also asks you to indicate if changes were made. This bed is edited: a 1–4 kHz dip, a level ride, and an end that cross-fades into a held tone. Add "Edited for this video", or "Changes: trimmed and mixed", under the credit.
- **Content ID.** The composer pre-registers his catalogue with YouTube Content ID (https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/youtube-contentid.html: "having the music pre-registered, it stops the baddies and bad data from claiming my music incorrectly"). A claim on a correctly credited video is possible and disputable. The page advises: "Be sure to add credits BEFORE YOU DISPUTE THE CLAIM", and says a disputed claim "will be released within 72 hours and likely much faster".
