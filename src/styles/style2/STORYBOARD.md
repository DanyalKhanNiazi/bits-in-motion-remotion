# Night Signal (Style 2): storyboard for the Ep01 intro

Composition `NightSignal`, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 1,890 frames (f0–f1889), muted. All frames are absolute, from
`public/data/ep01_words.json` through the kit (`ANCHORS`, `WORD_CUES`, `wordStart`), and live in
`timeline.ts` (`F`). The cue list in `cues.json` is generated from the same constants (`CUES`).

## The idea in one line

A short film shot inside a sleeping PC. Nothing glows unless it is powered or carrying a signal, so the viewer
reads the state before reading a word. Letterbox means "later"; full frame means now.

## The set and the cameras (one set, built once)

- **One set** (`set.ts`): a 380 × 468 mm mid-tower seen side-on (rear at left), micro-ATX board, LGA1700 CPU
  under a stock cooler drawn as dark glass, two DIMMs, M.2 SSD, empty x16 slot, firmware flash, chipset under a
  dark-glass heatsink with two illustrative blocks inside (the lit **lookout**, square; the dark **"?"**,
  wider), front-panel header, the button low on the front bezel, a bottom-mounted ATX supply with IEC inlet,
  seated plug and rear rocker, a PSU cover with grommet A, grommet B beside the 24-pin. Far plane: the wall
  with its socket and the cord (left), the desk and the black monitor (right).
- **Master W (re-framed, fix 1):** 2.3 px/mm, tower at x 570–1444 (shifted right of Stage A's frame), so the
  wall, socket and the whole cord read on the left and the board no longer fills Style 5's rectangle.
- **The iris (fix 1):** its canonical place is **on the chipset itself**: centre (1058, 660), d 300, ×4,
  a loupe over the part, so it needs no leader. Its view is revealed by a radial wipe that follows the ring's
  pen. Its tags sit in the lower-left board pocket (x 600–890), leaders crossing the ring. The ×4 chip sits at
  3:00 (4:30 would cover the button's lead).
- **Other setups** (`camera.ts`, cameras on the same set data): **C** CPU insert (×4.35 of W, package
  centred at x 910 so the board's left edge sits at x 111, left of the slate), **P** the relay (its own preview
  layer), **M** the screen (×2.6, monitor ~460 px and centred, the bezel a near silhouette at x 0–110, left of
  the slate), **E** establishing (×0.7), **K** plug insert (×1.9). C and M were re-centred in the review
  (their edges ran through the slate).
- **Planes:** far ×0.6 and stage ×1.0 in log space, so pull-backs are dollies (the far monitor shrinks least).
  Strokes are constant on screen at every scale (parts are re-drawn natively; nothing is scaled).
- **Tones** (`tones.ts`, mixes of brand tokens, no new hue): void #000 (bars, screen), far #0A0C15, tray
  #181D2A, room #1C2230 (board + 8 % structure), stage #1A1F2C, mass #121522, near #070911, glass (mass 40 %
  over stage) #171B28, lit #26282E, desk/floor #151A26, wash energy 16 % over stage. tray, room, mass and desk
  were lifted after measuring the H.264 encode (every idle 3 px outline stays at 3.0:1 or more).
- **Light rule:** powered parts = lit body + 4 px ink outline (+ the one faked glow on the part the voice is
  about); the Bit with its stepped halo and the 10-frame route light behind it; amber fills channels and never
  shines. Pointing at a wire is a one-step tone change (structure → textMuted), never light.

## Assets and how they are made

All art is original vector code; no bitmaps, stock, generated images or downloaded art. Text is live text.

| Asset | File | How |
|---|---|---|
| The set (case, supply, board, CPU, cooler, DIMMs, SSD, slot, flash, chipset, header, button, cables, room) | `set.ts`, `SetView.tsx`, `draw.tsx` | Part geometry in mm, drawn through a camera as flat planes with constant strokes, 2 px contact edges and dark glass |
| Cameras and moves | `camera.ts` | Per-plane similarity transforms; log-space zoom about each plane's fixed point; Move easing |
| Iris | `Iris.tsx` | Kit `lensState` timing; radial-wipe clip; D24 settle; ×4 chip at 3:00 |
| Bit and route light | `BitLayer.tsx` | Kit `bitRun` / `bitState` in W px, mapped through the camera at constant on-screen sizes |
| Tags and names | `Tag.tsx` | Brand keyed-tag anatomy with a masked reveal from behind the key bar; one or two lines |
| Slate | `Slate.tsx` | Time chip / scope header with masked vertical rolls; switches with cuts |
| Relay | `Relay.tsx` | Preview stations, hops, readied / lit / visited states, its own reveal camera |
| Screen fork | `ScreenFork.tsx` | Generic chips with vector glyphs (circular arrow, IEC power) |
| Stops | `Stops.tsx` | Unlit structure-line diagrams with vertical mask reveals |
| Analogy arc and card | `Analogy.tsx` | Dashed 12/8 annotation arc, kit `AnalogyFrame` + `ApproxChip` |
| Kit components used as-is | `src/shared` | `EnergyChannel`, `StateGlyph`, `EyeBadge`, `StatePill`, `Brackets`, `ApproxChip`, `AnalogyFrame` |
